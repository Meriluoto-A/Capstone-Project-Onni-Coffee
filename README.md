# Capstone-Project3-Coffee-Shop-Sales

Author: Anne M
Date: 4 September 2026

This Capstone Project for the Google Data Analytics Certificate course follows the six step data analysis process: 

1.	Ask – define the business problem
2.	Prepare – gather data
3.	Process – clean the data
4.	Analyse – analyse to find the insights
5.	Share – share the results
6.	Act – turn insights into decisions

## ASK

**BUSINESS TASK:**  
1. Performance Comparison Across Locations (New York, Paris, Tokyo): How sales performance and product mix differ across the three store locations. 
2. Product Category Profitability: Analyze which product categories (coffee, tea, bakery, chocolate drinks, syrups) drive the most revenue and margin across locations. 

**STAKEHOLDER:** Corporate Operations Manager who oversees performance across all store location

## PREPARE
- Dataset was taken from Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ahmedabbas757/coffee-sales
- Slight modification was made to the dataset and it was separated into three different excels.
- The dataset contains 149456 transactions (rows) in three coffee shops (Paris, Tokyo, New York) during 1/1/2023-30/6/2023.
